Frequently asked questions

What to know before we begin.

Have another question? Message us on WhatsApp →

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted according to its scope, structure, content and functionality. Once we understand what you need, we prepare a clear, tailored proposal with no obligation.

How long does development take?

Timing depends on the project’s complexity and the materials available. We agree on a realistic schedule before work begins and keep you informed throughout.

What do I need to get started?

A clear idea of what you want to achieve is enough. If you already have a logo, copy or photos we’ll put them to use; if not, we’ll guide you on what to prepare.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

Yes. If you’d rather we take care of it, your site is hosted and looked after under a monthly maintenance plan. If you’d rather do it yourself, we deliver it finished and help you get it published. The domain cost depends on the address you choose.

Will my website look good on mobile?

Yes. Every site is designed and tested for phones, tablets and desktops, with load times kept in check.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work remotely and can build your website in Spanish, English or Portuguese.

What happens after the website goes live?

Launch is the beginning of your online presence. You can add a monthly maintenance plan so your site stays online and running, and we quote edits, improvements and new features separately.
