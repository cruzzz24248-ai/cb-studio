Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope, including pages, galleries and functionality. Get in touch and we’ll prepare a tailored proposal.

How long does it take?

A simple website may take a few days, while a larger site can take a few weeks. We’ll confirm the timeline before work begins.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

We can deploy your website on a quality hosting platform. A custom domain is usually a separate annual cost, and we can help you choose and connect it.

Can I update photos and projects myself?

Depending on the project, we can set up a simple editing workflow so you can update content without touching code.
